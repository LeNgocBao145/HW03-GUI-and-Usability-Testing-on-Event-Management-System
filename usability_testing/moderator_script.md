# Moderator Script

## Opening

Thank you for taking part. We are evaluating the EMS interface, not you. There are no right or wrong answers. Please work as you normally would and say what you notice, expect, and find confusing. You may pause or stop at any time.

## Consent Reminder

Before we begin, please confirm that you consent to observation and collection of the session data described in the consent form. The repository will use only a masked participant ID; private contact and consent evidence will be stored separately.

## Think-Aloud Reminder

Please say what you are trying to do, what you expect to happen, and anything confusing along the way.

## Participant Task

"You want to attend an upcoming EMS event. Find a suitable event from the event listing, review its details, and complete the registration process using the attendee role you would actually use. Tell me when you believe the registration has been recorded."

## Neutral Prompts

- What are you thinking now?
- What would you expect to happen here?
- What makes that unclear?

## Closing

Thank you. The task is now complete. I will ask ten short SUS questions followed by eight open-ended questions about what felt clear or difficult. Your answers will be associated only with your masked participant ID in repository-visible files.

Stop the task timer before administering SUS. Record task-directed help as an intervention; record neutral think-aloud reminders separately.
