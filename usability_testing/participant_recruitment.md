# Participant Recruitment

## Target Profile

Suitable participants include:

- students
- lecturers
- event-goers
- people with typical web experience

Participants must:

- be real people
- be outside the class
- match the target user profile
- provide verifiable contact details
- consent to the session
- not be impersonated

## Required Sample

- 1 pilot participant
- 5 main participants

The pilot must not be counted among the required main participants.

## Privacy Rules

- Mask the middle four digits of phone or Zalo numbers.
- Mask personal email addresses where appropriate.
- Do not commit raw private contact details.
- Do not store passwords.
- Keep raw identifiable data private and outside the public repository.

Example masked phone or Zalo number: `090****789`.
