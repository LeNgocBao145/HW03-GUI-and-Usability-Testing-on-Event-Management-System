# Moderator Guide

## Before the Session

- Verify EMS availability without claiming the endpoint is operational before the check.
- Verify the participant test account and keep its credentials private.
- Verify that an upcoming event with open registration exists.
- Reset the participant starting state.
- Verify the timer and screenshot evidence setup.
- Prepare the observation notes.
- Confirm consent before collecting session data.

## Introduction Script

"Thank you for taking part. We are testing the product, not you, so there are no right or wrong answers. Please think aloud by telling me what you expect, notice, and find confusing. You may pause or stop the session at any time."

## Think-Aloud Guidance

Use neutral prompts only when needed:

- "What are you thinking?"
- "What do you expect to happen?"
- "What would you do next?"

Do not use leading prompts such as:

- "Click Filters."
- "The Register button is below."
- "Choose this role."

## During the Task

- Start the timer when the participant begins the task.
- Observe neutrally and avoid teaching the interface.
- Record errors using the agreed definition.
- Record hesitations using the agreed definition.
- Record every moderator intervention.
- Separate technical interruptions from participant behavior.
- Intervene only when the participant is completely stuck or safety and session integrity require it.

## Closing

- Stop the timer at the task endpoint.
- Record the outcome using the defined outcome categories.
- Administer all ten SUS items.
- Ask the post-task probe questions.
- Thank the participant.

## Intervention Rules

### Neutral Encouragement

A non-directive reminder to continue thinking aloud or describe expectations. It does not reveal a control, route, answer, or next step and is not counted as moderator intervention.

### Moderator Intervention

Any task-directed help that reveals where to act, what to select, or how to recover. Count and timestamp each intervention because it may affect the task outcome.

### Technical Assistance

Help used only to restore the planned test condition after a network, authentication, server, data, device, or screenshot-capture problem. Log it as a technical interruption, not automatically as participant error. Mark the session invalid if the interruption prevents reliable observation.
